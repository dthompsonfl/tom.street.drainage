# Printable Tom Street neighbor forms

These PDFs are **blank, public-safe master forms**, intended to be printed or saved for private household use. They contain no completed resident entries, photographs or official findings. Blank forms may be downloaded without permission; completed or annotated copies are private unless individually reviewed and approved for sharing.

Use US Letter, portrait, **actual size/100%** (not borderless). Black-and-white printing is supported. The complete packet has 18 deliberately single-purpose pages; print only the sheets needed. For extra writing space, add a continuation sheet labeled with the relevant event, photo or contact ID.

The reproducible source is [`../../tools/build_neighbor_toolkit.py`](../../tools/build_neighbor_toolkit.py). The PDFs are derived artifacts rebuilt by the repository's release workflow. The fact that a blank worksheet is available here is not approval of any submitted letter, factual statement, private disclosure or engineering conclusion.

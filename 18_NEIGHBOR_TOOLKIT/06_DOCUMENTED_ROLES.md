# Identify documented roles without assigning blame

A property or drainage system can involve different parties at different times. Use the [documented-roles worksheet](print/13_Documented_Roles.pdf) and the optional [blank tracker](templates/DOCUMENTED_ROLE_TRACKER.csv). **One name on a permit does not identify everyone responsible for every part of a drainage system.**

## Roles to check separately

| Role | Record that may help | What to keep open |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recorded property interest | Current/historical Property Appraiser records, deeds | Effective date and scope of an interest |
| Permit applicant / holder | Issued permit and approved revisions | Permit conditions and changes |
| Builder / performing contractor | Permit details, contractor record, work order | Who performed *this specific* work |
| Engineering designer | Signed/sealed approved drawing and revisions | Whether that drawing governs the feature |
| Plan reviewer / approving office | Review comments, approval stamp and date | Exact approval and any conditions |
| Field inspector | Inspection entry and written finding | Observation versus verification of completed work |
| Roadway / right-of-way office | County asset maps, right-of-way inventory | Who maintains *this particular* structure |
| Pipe, inlet, swale or outlet maintainer | Recorded easement, agreement, acceptance, maintenance inventory | Exact asset, boundaries and effective dates |
| Environmental permitting office, if applicable | Agency determination or authorization file | Whether its scope actually reaches this site |

**Record all three together:** the party or office *named in a record*, the **dated source ID**, and the **specific feature or work** covered. If there is no source, leave the responsible-party field blank and write what document is missing. A road shown as county-maintained does not by itself establish ownership or maintenance of each roadside pipe, ditch or inlet.

For the two construction parcels discussed in the [public evidence guide](../PUBLIC_EVIDENCE_GUIDE.md), archived records identify building permits. The currently missing approved grading drawings, measured pipe/inlet elevations, verified downstream outlet, construction completion records, actual easements and maintenance agreements are more important than an unsupported guess about who is responsible.

The role worksheet is **a question and source register**, not a conclusion. Keep different plan revisions, future responses and contradictory documents in the chronology rather than silently making them agree.

# Request County records and keep a follow-up trail

The project already has [unsent agency request drafts](../13_PUBLIC_RECORDS_REQUESTS/). You can adapt one to your own question and submit it through the agency's published process. Sending a public-records request under Florida Chapter 119 is different from asking Public Works to inspect or repair a drainage issue.

## Official Santa Rosa County forms

Use the [public-records request form](https://www.santarosa.fl.gov/FormCenter/Public-Record-Request-10/Submit-a-Public-Records-Request-55) to ask for existing plans and records. Use the separate [Citizen Work Request form](https://www.santarosa.fl.gov/FormCenter/Public-Works-7/Citizen-Work-Request-48) for a drainage inspection or maintenance concern. Both pages were checked as official County links on October 16, 2026.

**Neither request is considered submitted because a template exists here.** Only record the request as sent after you actually submit it and retain the acknowledgment or other reliable transmission evidence. Provide personal contact information on the County's own form, not in a public GitHub issue.

## After sending

1. Keep the original outgoing message and a complete copy of any attachment.
2. Record the agency, person or inbox contacted, the exact send date/time and any acknowledgment or request/case number.
3. Log the agency's response date, fees, records actually produced, redactions and whether the response says more records may follow.
4. Preserve delivered files unchanged and record their original filenames. Add your own notes separately.
5. If you receive no answer or an incomplete response, follow up with the same tracking number and the exact missing items.

Use [the request tracker](templates/REQUEST_TRACKER.csv). Its rows are blank on purpose. Do not put "submitted" just because there is a draft in GitHub.

**Example questions:** "Please provide the approved grading plan and any approved revisions for BP-2026-1621." "Please identify the County's existing roadside culvert inventory and relevant maintenance records for the Tom Street segment." Keep existing-record requests separate from asking the County to generate a new survey or opinion.

Do not promise yourself a particular legal deadline or agency finding based only on this guide. If the timing or refusal matters formally, verify the applicable law and communicate with the custodian.

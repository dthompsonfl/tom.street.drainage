# For Tom Street neighbors | Simple evidence toolkit

You do **not** need to know GitHub, write code or understand surveying to help. This folder contains blank forms you can copy onto your phone or print. The goal is to keep a clear, dated record of what you personally saw and what agencies actually said.

Before you begin, [read this short neighbor review guide](../NEIGHBOR_REVIEW_GUIDE.md). It explains how to give feedback without needing a GitHub account or uploading personal material.

## Three easy things to do

1. **Write down the observation.** What happened, where did you stand, what was the date/time, what was the weather and what could you see? Separate what you saw from what you believe caused it. Use [the observation guide](01_DOCUMENT_AN_OBSERVATION.md) or [blank event log](templates/OBSERVATION_LOG.csv).
2. **Save the original.** Keep photos, video and emails exactly as received in private storage. Do not crop, edit, rename away provenance or publish location/device metadata. A copy for discussion can be edited and labeled.
3. **Track who you contacted.** When you request County records or an inspection, keep the exact outgoing message, receipt, case number, replies, records received and next expected step. Use [the records guide](02_REQUESTS_AND_RESPONSES.md) and [blank tracker](templates/REQUEST_TRACKER.csv).

## If you are going to a meeting

Use the [meeting guide](03_MEETING_GUIDE.md). Bring the [brief](../15_RECONCILED_DRAFTS/01_HOMEOWNER_BRIEF.md), the appropriate **draft** letter, and a blank [meeting notes form](templates/MEETING_NOTES.md). Do not present the older withdrawn packet.

## If you want your information added to this public project

Read [privacy and sharing](04_PRIVACY_AND_SHARING.md) first. This is a **public** GitHub repository. Give originals and sensitive details privately to the authorized coordinator, then work together to create a source-linked, redacted public summary. No resident is required to disclose their name, home address or photo metadata publicly.

The coordinator uses the [correction form](templates/CHANGE_AND_CORRECTION.md) to record what changed, why and which source supports it. GitHub tracks versions of the public files. The private originals should remain in a separate permission-controlled evidence store.

## What these records do not prove on their own

A photograph or a puddle does not identify the upstream cause. Higher-looking fill is not a measured elevation. An inspector's direction is not proof that work was completed. We are gathering facts that County staff and qualified engineers can verify.

**Current status: community research and draft paperwork, not an official County conclusion.**

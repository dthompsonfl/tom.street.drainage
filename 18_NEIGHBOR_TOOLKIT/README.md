# Tom Street | Neighbor documentation toolkit

**A practical, printable set of blank worksheets for recording drainage conditions and official responses.** You do not need to know engineering, use GitHub, or fill out every page. These forms are for **your own observations and records**, not an official finding about anyone's actions.

## Start here: just three steps

1. **Write down what you saw.** Print the [one-page event form](print/03_Event_Observation.pdf) whenever you notice standing water, runoff, drainage work, or even normal dry conditions. Include the date, local time and time zone, safe viewing point, what you actually observed, and when you wrote the note. Use the [observation guide](01_DOCUMENT_AN_OBSERVATION.md).
2. **Save the originals.** Keep unchanged photographs, videos, emails, letters and downloaded records in your own private storage. Print the [photo/file index](print/04_Photo_and_Video_Index.pdf) if useful. Note original filenames and use a simple identifier such as `P-001`. Do not put completed forms or personal files into this public repository.
3. **Keep track of contacts.** When you contact an office, save the exact message, the date it was sent, any receipt/case number, what they replied, and what is still missing. Use the [contact form](print/10_Request_and_Response.pdf) and the [request guide](02_REQUESTS_AND_RESPONSES.md).

## Optional editable logs

For people who prefer spreadsheets, the [blank CSV templates](templates/) provide event, media, document, contact, work, and responsibility tracking. **CSV files do not secure private data. Never commit a filled-in log to this public repository.** The blank CSVs are templates, not evidence that any event, request, repair, or approval happened.

## How to describe a concern accurately

- **Observed:** "Water remained in the roadside swale at 4:10 PM, about one hour after rain ended." Record the location, event ID and the source of that rain timing.
- **Possible explanation:** "The grade change might affect runoff." This is a question, not a measured conclusion.
- **From a record:** "A dated inspection note discusses a proposed inlet." The note does not itself prove that the inlet was built, connected or operating.
- **Unknown:** It is appropriate to write "not measured," "I do not know," or "needs an agency response."

Include observations that do **not** support the concern: dry days, earlier ponding, repairs, unusually heavy rain, and different camera viewpoints. [Read the more detailed source and uncertainty guide](07_SOURCE_AND_MEASUREMENT_GUIDE.md).

## Help document who is responsible for what

Different people or organizations may have separate roles in property ownership, permit application, design, construction, inspections, roadway maintenance and individual drainage features. **Do not assign an organization to a role just because it appears on a neighboring record.** Use the [role guide](06_DOCUMENTED_ROLES.md) and the [printable role worksheet](print/13_Documented_Roles.pdf). Every entry needs a dated source and an exact feature or scope.

Saved County records identify permits for 2264 Tom Street (`BP-2026-1621`) and 2268 Tom Street (`BP-2026-1612`), but the approved lot-specific grading plans, actual drainage elevations, installed drainage configuration and structure-by-structure maintenance obligations remain open questions. Permit statuses in saved records must not be represented as current without a new check. [Public-safe source guide](../PUBLIC_EVIDENCE_GUIDE.md).

## Before you share anything

This is a **public GitHub repository**. Keep resident names, personal contact details, completed forms, original photographs, image GPS data, full email threads and other private material in protected storage. Share only separately reviewed copies with permission. [Privacy and sharing](04_PRIVACY_AND_SHARING.md) | [Originals and reference IDs](05_ORIGINALS_AND_IDS.md).

To request official records or a roadway inspection, use the [agency starting points](09_WHERE_TO_REQUEST_AND_REPORT.md).

For a County discussion, use the [meeting guide](03_MEETING_GUIDE.md) and [current homeowner brief](../15_RECONCILED_DRAFTS/01_HOMEOWNER_BRIEF.md). Any official letters in the separate draft folder remain drafts until the individual user checks and approves them. Older meeting packets are superseded.

**Toolkit scope:** blank resident worksheets, public-safe guides and optional blank spreadsheets. These do not establish a drainage deficiency, measured grade change, completed work, or the identity of a party responsible for a particular feature.

# Coordinator checklist | Keep neighbors' records usable and private

This is for someone combining information from several households. **The public repository is for blank templates and independently reviewed summaries, not for completed personal records.** Each household controls its originals and may decide whether a particular summary can be shared.

## Intake and organization

- [ ] Give each household a **private reference code**. A shared `E-001` is not globally unique until associated with that household's private code.
- [ ] Keep originals with the household or authorized private custodian. Record where they can be retrieved, who supplied them and any sharing restrictions.
- [ ] Keep an intake log with ID, source/author, original date, date received, type, protected location and optional hash.
- [ ] Check that the observer distinguishes firsthand events from later recollections, reports by others and possible causes.
- [ ] Connect each observation to any photos, events, records and limitations without rewriting the original account.
- [ ] Preserve contradictory reports, normal conditions, alternate causes, corrections and out-of-date source versions.
- [ ] Keep formal record requests **draft** until there is evidence of transmission. Track partial production versus completion and any reference number.
- [ ] For a described responsible role, require a **dated source and a specific feature/scope**. Mark unknown roles and missing drawings openly.
- [ ] Keep notes about actual work and follow-up conditions separate from a proposed fix or promise.

## Before preparing a public summary

- [ ] Identify the narrow question the summary answers and exactly which record/event supports it.
- [ ] Verify dates, source links, permit references and whether statuses came from an archived capture or current response.
- [ ] Confirm the resident approved their own first-person words and the intended publication scope.
- [ ] Remove private contacts, exact GPS, device metadata, raw email threads, unnecessary identifiable people and original files.
- [ ] Check the exported copy itself for embedded/hidden content, not just the editing screen.
- [ ] Record a second independent source/privacy review, the approval date and the published version.

## Project-specific unanswered questions

The current public summary still needs approved plans/revisions, comparable surveyed elevations, the installed drainage configuration and verified outlet, recorded drainage rights, structure-specific maintenance responsibility, and written inspection findings. **A blank worksheet cannot close any of those external evidence gaps.**

For an actual change to the public narrative, use [the correction template](templates/CHANGE_AND_CORRECTION.md) and follow [publication controls](../PUBLICATION_AND_VERSIONING.md). For an everyday neighbor, the [one-page observation form](print/03_Event_Observation.pdf) is sufficient to start.

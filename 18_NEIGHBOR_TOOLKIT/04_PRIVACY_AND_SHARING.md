# Private evidence and safe public summaries

**This GitHub repository is public. Treat any file or comment added here as permanently discoverable, including through prior versions.** Blank worksheets may be public; completed household records generally should not be.

## Keep these in private household-controlled storage

- Completed forms, names, contact details, private property descriptions and household statements.
- Original photos, video, Live Photo components and camera metadata, especially GPS or identifiable neighboring properties.
- Unredacted emails, messages, receipts, invoices, visitor details and full meeting attendance lists.
- Authenticated portal captures, cookies, passwords, login or session files.
- Originals supplied by another person unless you have documented permission for the intended use.

**Preserve originals unchanged.** Record where each is stored, the source, when it was acquired and an optional SHA-256 hash. Make a distinct copy for annotation or redaction. An edited image is not the original, even if the change seems minor. Simply drawing an opaque-looking rectangle over a PDF may not actually remove its hidden underlying text or metadata.

## A safe sharing process

1. Decide exactly **which information** is necessary for the question being discussed.
2. Obtain permission from any resident whose own information or first-person words will be shared. Record the scope and date of that permission privately.
3. Prepare a **separate public-safe summary**: source or evidence ID, general location, record/event date, accurate description, what it does and does not establish, and missing information. Do not attach private originals to a public issue.
4. Check visible content **and embedded information** (metadata, document properties, hidden text, filenames, GPS and attachments) in the final exported file.
5. Have someone else check that dates, permit IDs, source references, uncertainty and permissions are correct before publishing. Keep a private record of the approval and which version was shared.

**A populated CSV is not protected simply because it is called a template.** Keep copies of all completed spreadsheets away from the public repository. If a private file is accidentally published, stop sharing its link and document the exposure before any cleanup; deleting the latest version may leave earlier copies accessible.

See [originals and IDs](05_ORIGINALS_AND_IDS.md), [public version controls](../PUBLICATION_AND_VERSIONING.md) and the packet's private sharing-review page 17. The existence of a public blank form does not authorize publishing someone else's completed entry.

# Where to request records or report a drainage concern

These are official Santa Rosa County pages, checked October 16, 2026. **A records request and a road/drainage service report are different actions.** The County cannot provide another agency's records simply because the location is in Santa Rosa County. Confirm the current process on the linked agency page before sending.

## If you want existing County records

Use the [Santa Rosa County public-records information page](https://santarosa.fl.gov/485/Public-Records-FOIA-Requests) to find its current request method. Ask for an identifiable record, such as a dated approved plan, review note, inspection log, work order, maintenance inventory or recorded decision. The County explains that Chapter 119 requests need not use a particular form and do not require a name or reason; the relevant custodian may ask for enough information to locate the records. Fees or processing time can vary. Record the actual request, its date, and any receipt before marking it sent.

## If you want the roadway or drainage checked

Use the [Santa Rosa County Public Works road issues page](https://santarosa.fl.gov/377/Road-Issues) to find its current Citizen Work Request path. The County describes this service for pavement, culvert and drainage concerns on County-maintained roads. Its [Public Works page](https://santarosa.fl.gov/292/Public-Works) also explains how to check the road maintainer and find the appropriate contact if it is not a County-maintained segment.

Save the exact submitted report and confirmation number, and document any subsequent visit or response. A case being opened or closed is not, by itself, an engineering determination or proof of a completed repair.

## If you need recorded property instruments

The [Santa Rosa County Clerk's official site](https://santarosaclerk.com/) is a separate office. Use its current records/recording or public-request process for deeds, recorded plats and easements; don't assume the County commission's records form covers Clerk-held instruments.

## Use the information responsibly

Give exact permit numbers, date ranges, parcel references or a road segment only when you have checked those identifiers. The currently saved research records associate 2264 Tom Street with `BP-2026-1621` and 2268 Tom Street with `BP-2026-1612`; those are archival identifiers, not a real-time permit-status check. Ask for existing documents separately from requesting a new field inspection or survey.

After contact, fill out the [request and response sheet](print/10_Request_and_Response.pdf) and keep the outgoing message, receipts and received documents unchanged in private storage. [Tracking guide](02_REQUESTS_AND_RESPONSES.md).

# County meetings, site visits and neighborhood discussions

You do not need technical vocabulary to ask a useful question. Bring the [current homeowner brief](../15_RECONCILED_DRAFTS/01_HOMEOWNER_BRIEF.md), a few clearly labeled photos or a short description of firsthand observations, and blank [meeting or inspection notes](print/11_Meeting_and_Inspection_Notes.pdf) (packet page 11). Do not use superseded meeting packets.

**Suggested opening:** "I live near the construction on Tom Street, and I am trying to understand where rainwater is expected to go. Could the County help identify the approved drainage plans, inspect the conditions and explain who maintains the relevant structures?"

## Questions that produce something verifiable

- Which **approved plan set**, drawing number, date and revision apply? Were any changes or exceptions approved in writing?
- Does the available record include a **measured** road, swale, inlet, pipe-invert and receiving-outlet profile? What is the measurement datum? If not, what work is needed to establish it?
- What is the identified water route and downstream receiving point? What remains unconfirmed?
- For each particular ditch, pipe, inlet or outlet, what document identifies its **owner, construction responsibility and ongoing maintenance**?
- Will a qualified County contact arrange a documented site visit? What is the **case number**, who will coordinate, and when might written findings be available?

## During and afterward

Record the meeting date/time, attendees and roles, specific question asked, and the name or office providing each answer. **Label direct quotes** separately from your paraphrase. A proposed installation is not confirmation that it was completed; an inspection recommendation is not an approved plan. If a date or document was promised, write who agreed to provide it and when.

Afterward, send a short summary to the relevant office and invite corrections. Keep both outgoing and incoming copies. Do not state someone attended, promised or approved something unless the record supports it.

If speaking for another household, confirm permission first. Completed notes, personal contacts and original files remain private until individually reviewed for sharing. [Request guide](02_REQUESTS_AND_RESPONSES.md) | [documented roles](06_DOCUMENTED_ROLES.md).

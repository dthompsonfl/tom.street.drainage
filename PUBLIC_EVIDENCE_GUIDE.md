## The three missing pieces that matter most

1. **Approved drawings:** The plans and revisions actually governing the lots, including written exceptions.
2. **Measured conditions:** Comparable surveyed elevations of finished lots, roads, ditches, pipe inverts, inlets and the downstream outlet.
3. **Responsibility and response:** County asset records, drainage maintenance agreements, a documented field inspection and written findings.

A County inspector's instruction is not a completed work record. A field marked Assigned is not proof that a certification is missing. An empty online attachment list is not a County no-records answer. Regional imagery and an ordinary photograph are not engineering measurements.

## What to do with this information

Use [the homeowner packet](15_RECONCILED_DRAFTS/README.md) to explain the concern and [the neighbor toolkit](18_NEIGHBOR_TOOLKIT/README.md) to document what happens near your home. If you receive a new official record, preserve the exact original privately, document when it arrived and propose a **redacted** update to the public source index. Do not replace an older agency record silently; each record needs its date and source.

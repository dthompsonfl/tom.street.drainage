## Additional confirmed LABINS imagery index lead (October 17, 2026)

The exact **LABINS/FREAC State Plane North imagery grid ID `648138`** was subsequently spatially verified for the representative 2264, 2265, 2268 Tom Street locations and corridor center. Original MrSID images for 2007, 2010, 2013, 2016 and 2019, with available worldfiles and vendor metadata, were **actually downloaded and archived**; only a published 2016 `.aux` companion yielded HTTP 404. A full source-hash inventory is in `00_CONTROL/LABINS_2026-10-17_ORIGINAL_SOURCE_REVIEW.md`. **Do not request or charge for duplicate copies of those already obtained LABINS originals.** The critical imagery request remains the **2022 and 2025 site-covering FDOT source frames, exact capture dates and any alternate County archive**, whose availability at Tom Street has not been proven. The LABINS cell ID is a distinct system from FDOT APLUS frame identifiers.

The official LABINS Santa Rosa State Plane North imagery-grid PDF is now archived at `11_HYDROLOGY_AND_HAZARD/SRC_PUBLIC_GIS_2026-10-17/LABINS_SANTA_ROSA/ORIGINAL_LABINS_SANTA_ROSA_HIRES_GRID_MAP.pdf`. An FSU FREAC LABINS map source independently identifies the State Plane North imagery grid as layer 14 of `https://maps.freac.fsu.edu/arcgis/rest/services/LABINS/LABINS_Data_ccr_relate/MapServer/`, with `spn_id` tile identifiers. These are **grid references**, not photographs or evidence of County imagery coverage for an exact acquisition year. FDOT may use a different aerial indexing system, so please use the coordinates independently where necessary.

The public LABINS imagery inventory returned indexed Santa Rosa rows for 2007, 2010, 2013 and 2019 with flight-period metadata, but the bounded 2022 filtered response returned no row and the 2025 year was not selectable in that interface. This does **not** refute FDOT's separate county-level 2022/2025 collections.

## Chain-of-custody handling for received files

When an agency responds, preserve the complete original email/production notice (with any private contact details redacted from public release), original filenames and untouched electronic bytes in authorized evidence storage. Record receipt in the repo's `00_CONTROL` evidence/source ledgers; calculate SHA-256; record the issuer, issued/retrieved dates, official identifiers, storage path and property applicability. **Do not publish a private homeowner's personal data or unredacted resident JPEGs in the public repository.**

**Priority for reducing uncertainty:** Approved site-specific grading plans and as-built elevations remain more decisive than another regional aerial image. The historical imagery assists reconstruction, but without measured elevations and an identified receiving outfall it does not determine stormwater compliance or causation.

## Records requested

1. **All recorded deeds, conveyances and other instruments** conveying, or
   encumbering, the lots described above — including the chain of title back
   through the mobile-home era (1996 for 2264 Tom St, 2000 for 2268 Tom St) and
   forward to the present owner, Holiday Builders, Inc.
2. **All recorded subdivision plats** for Crescent Shores (00780), Pope
   Subdivision and Hadleigh Hills, and any plat amendment, correction, replat
   or affidavit of plat.
3. **All easements**, and specifically all **drainage easements, drainage rights
   of way, stormwater easements, common-area ownership records and joint
   cross-access easements** affecting the lots fronting Tom Street.
4. **All right-of-way dedications, road easements and public rights-of-way**
   for Tom Street.
5. **All lot division instruments, lot splits, parcel splits, boundary
   adjustments or replatting instruments** affecting the two lots, including any
   instrument that created or renumbered lots 1020 and 1490.
6. **All restrictive covenants, declarations, protective covenants, articles of
   incorporation, association governing documents or community standards**
   containing drainage, fill, grading, setback or stormwater provisions that
   bind either lot.
7. **All development agreements, impact-fee agreements, hold-harmless
   agreements, drainage maintenance agreements or assignments of drainage
   responsibility** relating to the Tom Street frontage.
8. Any recorded **notice, lis pendens, memorandum, affidavit or notice of
   violation** relating to drainage, grading or fill on either lot.
9. A **copy of the Clerk's index entries** covering the above categories, so
   the completeness of this production can be assessed.

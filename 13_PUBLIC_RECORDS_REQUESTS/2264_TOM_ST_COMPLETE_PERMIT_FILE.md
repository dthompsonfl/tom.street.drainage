## Records requested

Please produce existing records — not new analyses — in their original
maintained electronic format (PDF, CAD, DWG, DXF, GIS shapefile/geodatabase,
spreadsheet, or native image) where that format is releasable.

**A. All applications and permits of every category**
1. Every building permit application and issued permit for 2264 Tom Street, all
   permit types and all separate trades.
2. Every related application regardless of permit type, including but not
   limited to: grading, site work, clearing, land development, stormwater,
   erosion and sediment control, driveway, right-of-way, paving, plumbing,
   mechanical and electrical.
3. All linked, parent, child, associated, amended and revision applications.

**B. Plans and surveys**
4. Approved residential site plan / plot plan.
5. **All versions of the approved grading plan**, including the original and
   every revision, with each revision date and revision-cloud description.
6. Foundation plan and site elevation drawings; finished-floor elevation.
7. Existing spot elevations and proposed spot elevations.
8. Lot drainage arrows, flow paths, swale and drainage details.
9. Cut/fill quantities and any grading exception, waiver or variance.
10. Existing-conditions survey, topographic survey and boundary survey.
11. Engineer's grading certification and any soils or geotechnical report.
12. Construction erosion-control plan and sediment-control plan.
13. Temporary best-management-practice drawings and stormwater plan.

**C. Right-of-way and driveway**
14. Driveway permit, driveway plan and culvert installation details, including
    culvert size, invert elevations and any drainage-structure schedule.
15. Any right-of-way construction permit affecting Tom Street frontage.

**D. Review, inspection and closeout**
16. All plan-review comments, requests for corrections and resubmittals.
17. Complete inspection history, including failed inspections, correction
    notices and re-inspections, for every permit type.
18. Final inspection results and final approvals.
19. Certificate of Occupancy or Certificate of Completion, if issued.
20. **Certified as-built survey and final grading certification.**
21. Any notice of violation, code enforcement case, stop-work order or
    enforcement correspondence relating to this property.

**E. Index and metadata**
22. A document index or attachment listing for each permit record, showing

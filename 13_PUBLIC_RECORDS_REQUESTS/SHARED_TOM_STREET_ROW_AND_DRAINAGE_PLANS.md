## Why these records are being requested

County inspection records contain useful **observations and work instructions**, but do not yet establish the geometry, completion or legal responsibility for a shared drainage system:

- On **2026-08-06**, the driveway line-and-grade inspection for 2264 Tom Street
  was passed with the instruction: *"Use 100 ft of 12 inch ADS perforated pipe
  with sock; Tear out existing concrete end @ address 2260 and tie into with new
  pipe; Install 1st inlet box @ roughly halfway; Tear out existing concrete end
  @ address 2270 and install an inlet box."*
- On **2026-08-31**, the site grading / drainage pre-construction inspection for
  2268 Tom Street was passed with: *"Site passes. Ensure that roadway and
  stormwater ditch remain free of construction sediment."*
- On **2026-09-10**, the driveway line-and-grade inspection for 2268 Tom Street
  was passed with: *"Flat; Match Existing roadway Grades."*

These comments **refer to** a roadway ditch and proposed connections to structures associated with nearby addresses. They do **not** prove what was installed, where structures sit relative to the right-of-way, the system's hydraulic adequacy or ownership. Please confirm **whether the County holds** approved plans, profiles, reviews and specifications; the customer-facing attachment endpoint returned no files.

## Records requested

**Right-of-way construction**
1. All Right-of-Way Construction Permits for Tom Street in the Crescent Shores
   (00780) / Hadleigh Hills area, and all related approved drawings.
2. All Tom Street roadway profile drawings, cross-sections, typical sections,
   and **road crown, centreline and shoulder elevation data**.
3. As-built roadway plans for Tom Street, including any later widening,
   overlay or shoulder modification.

**Driveways and culverts**
4. All driveway permits for **2200 – 2300 Tom Street**, both even and odd sides,
   including permit numbers, approval dates and approved driveway plans.
5. All culvert installation records: **culvert sizes, materials, invert
   elevations, slope and length**, for every driveway crossing on the block.
6. Records of the **concrete drainage structures / "concrete ends"** referenced
   at addresses 2260 and 2270 Tom Street, including their invert elevations and
   as-built location.
7. Any **inlet box, catch basin or storm drain** records on the Tom Street
   frontage, including clean-out and maintenance history.

**Swales and drainage master plan**
8. The **subdivision drainage master plan** and approved grading master plan for
   Crescent Shores (00780) / Hadleigh Hills, all phases, including revisions.
9. Standard and typical **swale sections**, with bottom width, side slopes,
   centreline and invert elevations and design flow depth.
10. Any **culvert schedule, drainage structure schedule and receiving outfall
    designation** for the Tom Street drainage system.
11. Hydraulic and hydrologic studies, runoff calculations and any detention or
    attenuation analysis associated with the development.
12. Drainage easements, drainage rights-of-way, and any restrictive covenant
    affecting drainage or fill on the lots fronting Tom Street.
13. **Engineer's certifications** for the as-built subdivision drainage and
    roadway systems.

**Maintenance and complaints**
14. Public Works maintenance and mowing/drainage records for the Tom Street
    roadside ditch and swales.
15. Any drainage complaints, flooding reports, washout or ponding complaints
    recorded for Tom Street, and any resulting work orders or repairs.
16. Roadway maintenance acceptance documentation for the Tom Street drainage
    improvements.

**ERP coordination**
17. Any County-held copy of, or correspondence with NWFWMD concerning,
    **ERP 9220-2 (Hadleigh Hills, Phase 2)**, including the approved ERP
    stormwater drawings and the County's own acceptance of the drainage
    facilities that credit it.

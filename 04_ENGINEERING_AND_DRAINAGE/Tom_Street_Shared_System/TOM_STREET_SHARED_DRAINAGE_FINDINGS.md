## What the county records actually show

**Roadside ditch:** The archived August 31, 2026 inspection for 2268 mentions keeping a roadway/stormwater ditch clear of construction sediment. The statement does not quantify capacity, establish observed blockage, or prove that sediment left a construction lot.

**Pipe and inlet work:** The August 6, 2026 2264 driveway consultation contains an instruction for approximately 100 feet of 12-inch ADS perforated pipe and new inlets near drainage structures associated with 2260 and 2270. This is an **instruction**, not proof of an engineered approved design, installed alignment, easement rights, completed connection, actual invert elevations, or adequate system capacity.

**Roadway-grade instruction:** The September 10, 2026 2268 driveway consultation directs matching the existing roadway grade. The remark does not establish finished-floor elevation, numeric driveway/road elevations, or lack of freeboard.

**Older driveway inspections:** Archived driveway permit records include failures followed by passes. Failure counts alone do not establish the nature of defects or a current drainage problem. Verify the associated parcels before drawing conclusions about the connection locations.

**Drainage as-built tasks:** Both current-site archived MGO task lists include `Certified Drainage as Built` with state `Assigned`. This proves only that a workflow item existed at capture time. **Whether a signed document exists, was submitted, accepted, waived, due or overdue is NOT DETERMINED.** Open CO-related tasks likewise do not establish current CO issuance status after the capture date.

## Materials requested for preservation

1. **Florida Supreme Court corrected opinion**, *Westland Skating Center, Inc. v. Gus Machado Buick, Inc.*, 542 So. 2d 959 (Fla. 1989). Official court-image scan preserved by the Florida State University College of Law library. Source: https://library.law.fsu.edu/Digital-Collections/flsupct/dockets/72492/op-72492.pdf. Its reasonable-use legal framework is relevant background; later treatment and facts of these lots require independent review.

3. **2024 Q1 Florida DEP Final Order Index**. Source: https://floridadep.gov/sites/default/files/Final_Order_Report_-_January_1,_2024_-_March_31,_2024.pdf. On page 2, lists `OGC 23-1387`, `DEP24-0006`, “HOLIDAY BUILDERS INC.; DEP VS.”, Santa Rosa County, dated January 10, 2024.

**The two DEP index reports are NOT the actual signed final orders.** The subject matter codes in the listings and any separate 2023 mitigation-ledger association do not establish relevance to the 2026 Tom Street projects. Do not submit the orders as allegations without actual instrument text, project-site identification and comparison to the permits involved.

# Attributed statements and public edition approval | 2026-10-16

The [version-specific approval register](ATTRIBUTION_AND_PUBLICATION_APPROVAL.csv) records every active first-person guide or meeting draft plus a superseded letter. Values in that register are **approval requests only**, not signatures, reviewer confirmations, or publication consent. The stored checksum identifies a draft and must be rechecked if the wording changes.

## Human workflow

1. Give the original resident/speaker the exact dated SHA-256 version, intended audience, claim source notes and an opportunity to correct the language. Do not infer author approval from use of a name, address or first-person draft.
2. Keep signed/written permission outside public Git. Record a **non-personal approval reference**, UTC decision time, approver role, document hash, scope and any restrictions in a current register entry. If declined or changed, hold that version and issue a new review request; old signatures do not cover edits automatically.
3. Separate factual records from firsthand statements, attributed secondhand comments, inference, unsettled questions and verified professional findings. An inspector's instruction is not a built as-built; a GIS road classification is not ownership of every culvert; historical terrain is not a surveyed 2026 grade.
4. A different source/privacy reviewer checks each included source, contact field, embedded PDF/metadata, agency/public-access class, reuse terms and any plausible contrary evidence. Record unresolved terms; do not call an automated screen an approval.
5. Publication approver names **one immutable edition ID and contents checksum** and states its permitted distribution. The coordinator records decision and release date. A later edit is a **new edition**, not an overwrite of an old ZIP.
6. For an actual outgoing letter, the signatory personally signs and privately retains exact outgoing copy and agency receipt. Neighbor fact-checking of a DRAFT does not mean it was sent.

All currently active personal/speaker approvals remain pending. The five-page packet is an unsigned editorial draft; the official County originals remain credited to their agencies and are never converted into a resident's firsthand account. A future app must import decisions with stable approval IDs and evidence references, not issue numbers.

## Scope decision

These identifiers come from preserved County records, not a live status guarantee. Historical use of **2266 Tom Street** in MGO remains unresolved and must not be silently treated as a present parcel address.

**Local government references must use Santa Rosa County exclusively.** The Santa Rosa County Land Development Code, Santa Rosa County permit/engineering/inspection process, Santa Rosa County Property Appraiser, and Santa Rosa County Clerk of Court are the relevant local authorities. Do not borrow another Florida county's ordinances, drainage criteria, building department contacts, review standards, plats, parcel databases, or road-maintenance assignments. Florida statewide and federal statutes, rules, standards and records remain in scope when independently applicable. A regional agency operating across county boundaries (such as NWFWMD) is a **state/regional authority**, not a second local county code.

## Verified local text and edition control

**Primary county source:** [Santa Rosa County official Land Development Code](https://www.santarosa.fl.gov/DocumentCenter/View/5820/Santa-Rosa-County-Land-Development-Code-), preserved unchanged at [SOURCE/santa_rosa_county_LDC.pdf](../08_LEGAL_AND_REGULATORY/SOURCE/santa_rosa_county_LDC.pdf). The 509-page PDF's cover/revisions identify **November 10, 2025 / Ordinance 2025-25**. The PDF's **January 13, 2026 creation metadata is not a legislative effective date**.

Relevant published sections (the provided PDF is the evidence for the text, not proof of current site compliance):

- **3.04.04:** prohibition on obstructing natural or constructed drainageways. The actual drainageway and any obstruction must be demonstrated.
- **3.04.05:** erosion and sediment controls; SWPPP and federal/DEP permit applicability remain fact dependent.
- **4.04.01.A-C:** drainage/stormwater management requirements and professional plans subject to exemptions and approval circumstances.
- **4.04.01.D.1:** conditional lot-grading-plan pathway for specified sub-acre residential lots **without** an on-file subdivision lot grading plan. It identifies a signed/sealed Florida-PE plan or NWFWMD ERP letter alternative and the needed elevations/outfalls/flow mapping, plus a completion-stage engineer letter for that pathway. **Whether the County has a subdivision plan on file is unknown.**
- **4.04.01.D.4:** possible lot-grading-plan exemptions; no exemption has been established or ruled out for either lot.
- **4.04.01.E:** separately states **all lots require a certified as-built prior to Certificate of Occupancy** in the published edition. County task status “Certified Drainage as Built: Assigned” is **not evidence** that a signed document exists, is absent, was approved, or is overdue.
- **4.04.01.G-H:** ditch/swale and slope specifications are potential plan-review questions, **not a field determination** without measured cross-sections and an applicable approved design.

**Time-version gap:** See the [Santa Rosa 2026 Ordinances register](https://library.municode.com/fl/santa_rosa_county/ordinances/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=2026). It lists **2026-03** (adopted **January 22**, wellfield overlay changes) before the saved March 23 permit issuances, and **2026-10** (May 21), **2026-14** (June 22) and **2026-16** (July 23) as later zoning/future-land-use amendments. Their enactment notices **do not by themselves verify** whether any Tom Street map designation or the quoted drainage text changed. Acquire enacted ordinance PDFs, exhibits, effective-date clauses and parcel mapping, and obtain County confirmation of which version controlled each initial approval and later revision. Do **not** treat the 2025 cover as proof that no 2026 amendments exist.

## State and federal verification gates

- **Public records:** [Fla. Stat. 119.07 (2026)](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/119.07) is the legal basis for requests. No County, Clerk, FDEP or NWFWMD request in this project has a verified submission receipt. Request existing records, fees in advance, nonexempt portions and custodians; do not demand new calculations under Chapter 119.
- **ERP:** [Fla. Stat. 373.413 (2026)](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/373.413), [373.4131](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/373.4131), and Florida Administrative Code **62-330** provide statewide framework. The statutes include amendments that may postdate the March 2026 permits. A parcel-specific ERP requirement, exemption, grandfathering or parent-development authorization needs written agency documentation.
- **Construction stormwater:** Florida DEP's [Construction Activity](https://floridadep.gov/water/stormwater/content/construction-activity) and [Permits and Forms](https://floridadep.gov/water/stormwater/content/npdes-stormwater-permits-and-forms) pages identify **62-621.300(4)** and state that CGP coverage depends on a qualifying disturbed area **or common development plan**, together with qualifying discharge. Determine operator, site/common-plan footprint, discharge and coverage from actual records before concluding CGP applicability. Federal NPDES/Clean Water Act authority does not substitute for this evaluation.
- **FEMA/NOAA:** Keep dated flood-layer extracts and rain-source provenance. FEMA regulatory zones do not model every roadside swale blockage, localized inundation, or lot-to-lot runoff alteration. Hydrologic simulation or causation conclusions require surveyed inputs and qualified review.

6. Homeowner consent and native photo provenance before any public release; prior archived photo copies are not automatically originals or geolocated construction proof.

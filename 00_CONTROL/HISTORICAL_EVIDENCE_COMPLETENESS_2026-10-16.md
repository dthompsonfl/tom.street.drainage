## High-priority external gaps: do not call the archive exhaustive

1. **Existing County-held original approvals**: stamped lot-grading/site/drainage plans and every approved amendment for `BP-2026-1621` and `BP-2026-1612`, subdivision or parent/plat plans, any individual-lot grading exemption/engineering review scope, approved runoff design and drainage calculations.
2. **Recorded documents and responsibility**: original recorded plats, deeds, drain easements, road acceptance and maintenance agreements; ownership of roadside inlet/ditch, 12-inch pipe(s), driveway culverts and receiving outfall.
3. **Current and historic measured conditions**: any available certified as-builts, preconstruction boundary/topographic surveys, benchmark/datum records, 2026 survey of finished/adjacent lot elevations and actual upstream/downstream invert/outfall levels. A newly conducted survey is a service request, not an existing-records request.
4. **East Navarre analysis**: full report/status, hydrologic and hydraulic models and source DEM, field-survey/infrastructure inventory, complaint records, project schedules and local subbasin mapping. A public overall study-area image cannot substitute for these.
5. **Historic photo originals**: FDOT APLUS retrieval/coverage checks for 1966/1978/1988/1997 and other unacquired listed years; original orthorectified aerial TIFF/JP2 with actual acquisition-day metadata; county 2022 orthophoto, and 2024–26 imagery if held. High-level services often display archives but do not provide actual bytes.
6. **Environmental applicability**: full relevant NWFWMD ERP (including permit 9220-2 boundary) and construction CGP NOI/SWPPP authorizations/exemption determinations tied to the actual parcels and projects, not search radius alone.
7. **Private photo custody**: retrieve unmodified owner-authorized originals and sidecars, hash in private evidence vault, confirm actual capture dates and image directions. Do not publish personal geolocation, device identifiers or unrelated nearby residents in a public repo without review.
8. **Official work history**: original Road & Bridge foreman marked-up maps behind mapped problem area `2068`, complaint/repair/maintenance work orders and any inspection evidence for Tom Street before and during 2026.

The seven drafted Chapter 119 and service requests live in [`13_PUBLIC_RECORDS_REQUESTS/`](../13_PUBLIC_RECORDS_REQUESTS/). **Drafting a request is not submitting it**; do not mark a source obtained or a request submitted until a transmission receipt and the actual document are filed in the ledger.

### Suggested handling standards for future public records

- Retain immutable original bytes in `SOURCE/` or `ORIGINAL_*/`; record SHA-256, byte size, issuing agency, canonical URL, record ID, retrieval date/time UTC and license/distribution notes.
- Keep human-interpreted maps, photo annotations and coordinate transforms in a separate `DERIVED/` with inputs and method description.
- For environmental/survey evidence, record CRS, NAVD88/geoid, units, grid-cell spacing, survey date versus publication date, resolution, acquisition limits and source uncertainty.
- For resident originals, use private encrypted access-controlled storage and a public-safe index only. A commit to a public repository is an unauthorized distribution risk.
- An absence in an online search, a failed ArcGIS export, an unpopulated portal endpoint or an unsigned draft is **not** a certified agency no-records response.

**Conclusion:** Public digital-source acquisition is substantially advanced and has a reproducible set of original terrain bytes and government imagery. The case-specific approved engineering/recorded instruments, a current field survey, historic aerial frames from all published county years, and private photo rights remain unresolved. None should be described as complete until directly acquired and independently checked.

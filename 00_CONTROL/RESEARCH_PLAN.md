# Tom Street Drainage Investigation - Research Plan

## Project Overview
Investigation of potential drainage impacts from recent residential construction on Tom Street, Navarre, Santa Rosa County, Florida.

## Primary Addresses of Interest
- 2265 Tom Street (existing residence, occupied since late 2019)
- 2264 Tom Street (Site B - previously reported)
- 2266 Tom Street (possible construction site - requires verification)
- 2268 Tom Street (Site A - previously reported)

## Investigation Approach (Evidence-First)

### Phase A: Property & Parcel Research
- [ ] Search Santa Rosa County Property Appraiser (srcpa.gov)
- [ ] Search Santa Rosa County GIS (santarosa.fl.gov/470/Mapping-GIS)
- [ ] Search official recorded documents (acclaim.srccol.com)
- [ ] Verify parcel IDs and boundaries for all addresses
- [ ] Document legal descriptions and ownership history

### Phase B: Permits & Construction
- [ ] Search Development Services for building permits on each parcel
- [ ] Obtain grading, drainage, and engineering plans
- [ ] Review inspection records and certificates of occupancy
- [ ] Compare as-built requirements

### Phase C: Drainage System Reconstruction
- [ ] Research original subdivision drainage design
- [ ] Identify swales, culverts, easements, outfalls
- [ ] Determine maintenance responsibility
- [ ] Document pre-construction drainage patterns

### Phase D: Local Ordinances
- [ ] Obtain Santa Rosa County Land Development Code (current and historical versions)
- [ ] Review stormwater, grading, erosion control provisions
- [ ] Document effective dates

# Source access, privacy, attribution and reuse are different questions

**Dated 2026-10-16. No blanket relicensing of external records.** This policy classifies decisions that must be recorded for each file. It does not certify unrestricted reuse of anything in this public repository.

| Material | Access can be | Appropriate repository treatment | Permission not yet proven |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Santa Rosa County parcel and permit records | Anonymous public portal, ordinary free account, or official custodian response | Keep the authenticated **document** original unchanged, log source, acquisition and SHA; separately protect actual session artifacts | Embedded contact privacy, terms and redistribution |
| County Clerk Crescent Shores historical plat A/70 | Official public recorded-plat lookup (saved guest-search evidence) | Preserve original; use citations, agency name and precise book/page; historical map, not current survey | Specific reuse and visual metadata review |
| County GIS feature and road records | Public GIS service | Keep response and query URL, retrieval time, layer/object IDs and coordinate system | Present-day ground applicability or asset ownership |
| NOAA, USGS, USDA, FDOT, FDEP imagery and surveys | Agency download/portal | Record source/product/year, unit/CRS/datum, acquisition conditions, attribution and source limits | Product-specific terms and permission for redistributed derivatives |
| Public laws, opinions and agency reports | Official public sources | Preserve original versions and cite precise effective date/page/source, distinguishing binding text from interpretation | Third-party hosted copies, endorsements or relevance to these permits |
| Original blank household worksheet designs | Project-generated original work | Keep editable generator and dated original form; author/copyright owner decides whether and how to license future reuse | No license is granted solely by public Git visibility |
| Homeowner photos, private messages and completed forms | **Private owner-provided** | Originals outside Git, owner-specific consent and derived/redacted copy only when permitted | Public distribution, subject permission and EXIF/location disclosures |
| Reconciled homeowner narratives and packet PDFs | Project-authored derivative drafts | Version-specific author approval; source/claim checks; draft/status banner | Signed first-person approval, broader distribution approval |
| Historical superseded releases | Existing public Git history | Preserve without describing as current or endorsing earlier claims | Retroactive author approvals or corrected factual accuracy |

**Access classes:** anonymous agency public / ordinary free registration / custodian provided / public derivative / restricted household / actual session credential / unknown. Free registration alone must **not** trigger a confidential finding; conversely a public document may still have embedded fields that are unsuitable for a curated neighborhood packet.

**Reuse decision states:** `SOURCE_TERMS_REVIEW_PENDING`, `LINK_ONLY_WITH_ATTRIBUTION`, `REVIEWED_DERIVATIVE_PERMITTED`, `OWNER_CONSENT_REQUIRED`, `DO_NOT_REDISTRIBUTE`. A human reviewer records the basis and exact source/version before a state changes. Do not infer a license from file extension or a download button.

The [67-file technical review register](INDIVIDUAL_PRIVACY_TECHNICAL_REVIEW_2026-10-16.csv) identifies current machine flags without pronouncing them confidential or approved. The [historical Git assessment](HISTORICAL_GIT_EXPOSURE_ASSESSMENT_2026-10-16.md) separately addresses account artifacts that can remain reachable in commits. Preserve government originals and their checksums; issue separately versioned redacted derivatives only if justified.

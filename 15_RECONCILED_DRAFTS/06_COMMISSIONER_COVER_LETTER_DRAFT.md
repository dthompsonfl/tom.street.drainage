# Draft Letter to Santa Rosa County Commissioner

Date: ______________________________

To: **Santa Rosa County Commissioner**

**Re: Request for County engineering review and coordinated drainage response near 2264 and 2268 Tom Street, Navarre**

Dear Commissioner:

I am a nearby homeowner concerned about how ongoing construction and existing roadside drainage interact along Tom Street. I respectfully request your assistance in ensuring that County Engineering and Development Services review the matter together and provide residents a documented response.

The records available to us identify **2264 Tom Street** (parcel **21-2S-26-0780-0QQ00-1020**, permit **BP-2026-1621**) and **2268 Tom Street** (parcel **21-2S-26-0780-0QQ00-1490**, permit **BP-2026-1612**). Both permits showed **Active** in the October 2026 records we captured. County inspection comments mention planned pipe and inlet work near other Tom Street addresses, maintenance of the stormwater ditch, and matching roadway grades. We have not received the approved engineering drawings or measurements needed to evaluate the completed drainage.

Our concern is that any changes to lot grading, roadside conveyance or outfalls should be assessed for their effects on established neighboring homes. **We are not asserting that a law was violated or that either project caused flooding.** A higher new lot elevation, by itself, cannot answer those questions.

We respectfully ask your office to help arrange:

1. A named **County Engineering contact** and a coordinated review of both permits and the shared roadside drainage.
2. Production of the **approved grading/drainage plans**, revisions, survey and stormwater information applicable to each lot, including any subdivision-level plan.
3. A documented **field inspection**, with consideration of existing neighboring residences, driveway/culvert inverts, drainage outfall, asset-specific responsibilities along the GIS-listed County Maintained road, and any verified adverse impacts.
4. A **written summary of findings**, a tracking/reference number, and a clear follow-up timetable identifying any action needed and the responsible entity.

We understand that engineering judgments require actual plans, field measurements and qualified staff. Our request is simply that the relevant facts be examined before the issues become harder to document.

Thank you for your attention to our neighborhood.

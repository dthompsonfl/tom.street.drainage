# Tom Street drainage | Homeowner brief (DRAFT)

**October 16, 2026 | 2265 Tom Street, Navarre, Florida**

I am concerned about grading near the new homes across from mine and whether the roadside drainage will protect existing properties. Parts of the construction area appear higher than nearby homes. I do not have surveyed before-and-after elevations, so I cannot say how much the grade changed or whether construction has caused flooding.

- **2264 Tom Street:** parcel **21-2S-26-0780-0QQ00-1020**, permit **BP-2026-1621**.
- **2268 Tom Street:** parcel **21-2S-26-0780-0QQ00-1490**, permit **BP-2026-1612**.
- Both permits were marked **Active in saved October 2026 records**. That is not a current live-status check.
- Inspector comments refer to proposed 12-inch pipe and inlet work, keeping the roadside ditch clear of sediment and matching road grades. Comments are not approved plans or proof of finished construction.
- County GIS classifies this Tom Street road segment as **County Maintained**. Responsibility for each drainage structure is still unknown.

## What I am asking the County to do

1. Provide the approved grading and drainage plans, revisions, engineering review and any applicable exemptions for both lots.
2. Identify the measured road, ditch, inlet, pipe and receiving-outlet elevations and the actual route water takes.
3. Explain who owns and maintains the pipes, ditches and inlets.
4. Arrange a documented Engineering inspection and give neighbors a case number, written findings and next steps.

I am not asking for an assumption of fault. I am asking for the plans and measurements needed to tell whether the approved system protects the surrounding homes.

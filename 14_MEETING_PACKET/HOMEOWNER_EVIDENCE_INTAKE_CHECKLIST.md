# Homeowner Evidence Intake Checklist

This is a short intake form for gathering the one category of evidence that
**cannot be obtained from any government system**: your own dated observations
and photographs.

**Please keep the originals.** Do not crop, rotate, edit, re-save, or send them
through messaging apps that recompress images. Those apps strip GPS, timestamps
and camera metadata, which is often the only thing that makes a photograph
useful later.

## What to collect

| # | Item | Why it matters | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Photographs of standing or flowing water at the 2265 / 2264 / 2268 frontage | Establishes that a condition occurred at a place | Date, time, where in frame |
| 2 | The same view from a **second** vantage point (different angle, or the same spot from a second day) | Distinguishes a one-off from a recurring pattern | Two angles is much stronger than one |
| 3 | Photographs of the **roadside ditch / swale**, especially any sediment, vegetation blockage, or where it appears to stop | Directly tests the "roadway and stormwater ditch" the inspector referenced | Get close-ups and a wide shot |
| 4 | Photographs of the **2264 driveway construction**, including any open trench, pipe, or inlet box | Bears directly on whether the inspector's direction was followed | Date each shot |
| 5 | Photographs of the **2260 and 2270 drainage structures** (the concrete ends referenced by the inspector) | These are the tie-in points | Include a scale reference — a coin or a tape |
| 6 | Photographs of **new impervious surfaces** (driveways, roof downspouts, patios) as they appear | Relevant to runoff volume | Wide shot plus close-up |
| 7 | Photographs showing the **outlet point** — where ditch water actually goes | Tests positive outfall | Note if it's a swale, culvert, or unclear |
| 8 | A short written note per event: **what you saw, where, what time, how long it lasted, whether it was raining, and whether it had rained recently** | Observations without context are weak | Separate note per event |

## Metadata to preserve

For every photo and video, please record alongside it:

- **Date and time** of capture (confirm from the file's own metadata)
- **Device** used
- **Location** — which lot frontage or structure
- **Weather** — was it raining, and had it rained in the previous few hours?

## What we will do with it

- Keep the **original files untouched**, with a SHA-256 hash recorded for each.
- Create clearly labelled **derivatives** only if needed for sharing.
- Record the **capture date** and the **date we received it** as separate fields.
- Never post your photographs publicly without your say-so.

## Please do not

- Delete or "clean up" the originals after sharing
- Retouch, brighten, crop, or annotate the originals — we can derive annotated copies
- Post them in a public group or on social media without checking with us first
- Approach the builder or the neighbouring owners directly about this yet

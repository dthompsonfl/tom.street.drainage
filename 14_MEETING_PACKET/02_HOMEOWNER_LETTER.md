**Documents I am requesting**

Under section 119.07, Florida Statutes, I respectfully request copies of the
following **existing** records, in their original maintained electronic format

2. The **approved lot grading plan** for each lot, including every revision, with

3. The existing-conditions topographic survey and boundary survey, if any.
4. The erosion and sediment control plan for each site.
5. The driveway plan and culvert details, showing pipe size, material and
   **invert elevations**.
6. The **sealed engineering letter or certified drainage as-built** when it is
   submitted, or confirmation of when it is expected.
7. A copy of the County's document index for each permit file, showing every

8. Any code enforcement, stormwater, sediment-control or corrective-action file

**licensed contractor** for each permit.

1. Does the County Engineering Department have a **subdivision lot grading plan

2. Can the County confirm the **positive outfall and invert elevations** used for
   each driveway?
3. Is a **field inspection of the completed drainage** scheduled, and may I
   observe it?

I understand these requests may involve a fee. Please provide a **cost estimate

I would welcome a walk of the two driveways with the engineer of record once the
grading is complete, before the Certificate of Occupancy stage.

Current record, as verified from the County's own systems

I have confirmed through the Property Appraiser and the MGO Connect permit portal that Holiday Builders, Inc. is the owner of record of both lots and that two residential permits are active:

  • BP-2026-1621 — 2264 Tom Street, New Single Family (R), Active, issued 23 March 2026, 2,704 sq ft

I also note from the portal that both permits currently show the county task "Certified Drainage as Built" as Assigned, the mid-construction and final-construction site grading/drainage inspections as Not Complete, and "Issue CO" as Assigned.

Documents I am requesting

Under section 119.07, Florida Statutes, I respectfully request copies of the following existing records, in their original maintained electronic format where those formats are releasable (PDF, CAD/DWG/DXF, GIS, or spreadsheet):

  1. The approved residential site plan / plot plan for each permit.
  2. The approved lot grading plan for each lot, including every revision, with the date and description of each revision.
  3. The existing-conditions topographic survey and boundary survey, if any.
  4. The erosion and sediment control plan for each site.
  5. The driveway plan and culvert details, showing pipe size, material and invert elevations.
  6. The sealed engineering letter or certified drainage as-built when it is submitted, or confirmation of when it is expected.
  7. A copy of the County's document index for each permit file, showing every document the County holds — including documents that cannot be released, so that I can understand the full contents of the file.
  8. Any code enforcement, stormwater, sediment-control or corrective-action file relating to either lot.

I would also be grateful for the name of the engineer of record and the licensed contractor for each permit.

Three factual questions

  1. Does the County Engineering Department have a subdivision lot grading plan on file for either of these lots? Section 4.04.01.D.1 of the Land Development Code turns on this point for lots under one acre.
  2. Can the County confirm the positive outfall and invert elevations used for each driveway?
  3. Is a field inspection of the completed drainage scheduled, and may I observe it?

On cost and timing

I understand these requests may involve a fee. Please provide a cost estimate before incurring substantial charges, and I would be glad to accept rolling production in instalments rather than waiting for a single complete production. If any part of this material is withheld, please identify the specific statutory basis for the withholding and release the remainder, as section 119.07(1)(d) and (e) contemplate.

What I am not asserting

To be clear and fair: I am not claiming that any violation has occurred, that the drainage is inadequate, or that anyone has acted improperly. Both permits are active and the open drainage tasks are consistent with normal construction sequence. I am asking for the documents so that the question can be answered on the record rather than by assumption.

I would welcome a walk of the two driveways with the engineer of record once the grading is complete, before the Certificate of Occupancy stage.

Thank you for your time and your assistance. Please let me know if any part of this request needs clarification or if a different form is required.

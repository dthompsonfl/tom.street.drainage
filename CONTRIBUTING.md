# Contributing to the Tom Street drainage investigation

This is a **public evidence repository**, not a private document-submission channel. You do not need to be a developer to report an error. No contribution is accepted as verified evidence, a resident statement, or permission to publish merely because it is in a GitHub issue.

## Report a public correction or record lead

Use the [public record/correction issue template](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/record-lead.md). Include the **document and exact passage**, proposed correction, what is known versus uncertain, source agency and date, a **public source link**, and any evidence that contradicts the existing account. A broken link, outdated claim, missing source, uncertain interpretation, or a question about earlier drainage conditions is welcome. Clearly identify when a statement is an observation, a copied government record, an inference, or a question.

Do **not** upload homeowner photos, completed neighbor forms, contact lists, phone numbers, private correspondence, photo GPS/EXIF, cookies, tokens, authenticated portal captures, or payment information in an issue, PR, Discussion, or commit. GitHub issue content is public. Private submissions use the [controlled intake procedure](00_CONTROL/PRIVATE_INTAKE_AND_PORTABLE_METADATA_CONTRACT.md), only after the evidence owner and coordinator agree on the private transfer method. Never assume an issue is confidential.

## Changes to public material

1. Check [the current status](README.md) and source/claim register before proposing changes. Preserve originals unchanged and reference their source IDs, checksums, retrieval dates, and limitations.
2. Make corrections in the authoritative **editable source**, not a generated PDF or an older archive. Document corrections, older wording and contrary evidence in the appropriate current ledger.
3. Keep agency records in agency voice. First-person statements require the named author's personal confirmation of the **exact version**. Independent privacy/source review and an identifiable publication approver are separate gates.
4. Preview the changes, run applicable checks, regenerate derived files using their generators, and record what could not be verified. Treat old releases as fixed historical snapshots. Do not erase them or rewrite Git history to clean up the appearance of a problem.
5. Submit a reviewable change and wait for authorized integration. No automated workflow may push newly captured records or PDFs into `main`. Approval from CODEOWNERS is a routing rule, **not** proof of consent or independent review.

## Source and reuse permissions

Government accessibility, privacy, attribution, and redistribution rights are different matters. Free portal registration does not make an otherwise public agency record secret; equally, it does not establish blanket reproduction permission. See [reuse and provenance rules](00_CONTROL/REUSE_AND_RECORD_PROVENANCE_2026-10-16.md). Do not assume one license covers agency PDFs, aerials, historical plats, resident photos, and original blank worksheets.

The coordinator can correct an error, publish a superseding version after approval, or temporarily hold an unverified passage. The affected source stays in the appropriate protected evidence custody. The status register must show what changed, when, and why.
